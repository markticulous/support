A fast, private clipboard manager. Everything you copy, found again in a keystroke.

  • Opens anywhere with Ctrl+Alt+V. Type a few letters, press Enter, and it is pasted.
  • Your history is encrypted on your PC, and nothing leaves it.
  • Clips that look like passwords are not stored, and any app can be ignored.
  • Snippets, text transforms and text from images, right in the popup.
  • Updates itself quietly from GitHub.

Fast, private backup for Windows. Set it up once and it looks after your files.

  • Only changed files are read, and duplicate data is stored once.
  • Encrypted end to end. The format is documented, so a backup stays recoverable without Backee.
  • Works on every core at low priority, so the PC stays responsive.
  • A recovery USB restores a whole PC, even one that will not start Windows.

Deskside Radio

A desktop internet radio that looks like a radio.

  • Search for stations and see the song title and artist as they play.
  • Play schedules: the news at eight, jazz after lunch, off at midnight.
  • Eight themes, from an analogue dial to a console, plus special seasonal ones.
  • A watchdog reconnects a dropped stream on its own.
  • No account and no server. One double-click installs it and puts it on your desktop.

Tagee

Scannable QR tags for luggage, cases and stickers, so a lost thing can find its way home.

  • Whoever finds your item scans the tag and leaves you a message, with their location if they allow it.
  • A private dashboard to make, print and archive tags, and to keep the conversation.
  • Nothing about you is printed on the tag itself.
