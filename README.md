# Markticulous apps

The home page for my free apps: [Clipee](https://github.com/markticulous/Clipee), [Backee](https://github.com/markticulous/Backee), [Peekee](https://github.com/markticulous/Peekee), [Deskside Radio](https://github.com/markticulous/deskside-radio) and the ones still being built. Each app has a card, screenshots and its download links. It is served at **https://markticulous.github.io/support/**.

The apps are free, with no ads and no tracking, and nothing in them is locked. There is also a [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/markticulous) link for anyone who would like to chip in with a donation; nothing is sold here.

- `index.html`: the page. A Midnight hero, a card per app, then a profile section per app. Each profile shows a screenshot from `assets/<app>-screenshot.*`: a 16:10 JPG fills the frame; a whole window (PNG with rounded corners) goes in a `shot window` frame and sits centred on it.
- `supporters.json`: names of supporters who chose to be listed.
- `assets/`: logos, wordmarks and the Fraunces and Oswald fonts (SIL Open Font License, see `assets/OFL-Fraunces.txt` and `assets/OFL-Oswald.txt`).
